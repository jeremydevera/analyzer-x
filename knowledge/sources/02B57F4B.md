## Claims we could test on our own data

The page makes almost no claim that a crypto backtest could settle. Two things could be tried, both from the excerpts and both only as exercises:

1. Historical VaR from the k-th heaviest loss (lessons 1 and 2): take this repo's crypto daily returns (or our own trade profits per day), estimate a 95% or 99% VaR from the k-th worst loss over a past window, then count how often the next days' real losses go past it on days not used for the estimate. If the exceedance rate is far from 5% or 1%, the method fails on this data. The excerpt gives no k and no result, so this tests our own use of the idea, not the author's claim.
2. Fixed fraction in a risky asset, the rest in a safe one (lesson 4): on BTC or other coins, hold a fixed fraction of the account in the coin and the rest in cash, rebalanced each period, and compare final wealth and worst drawdown for several fractions. The excerpt states no result, so nothing is confirmed or refuted by the page itself.

The headline "Crypto Trading Was the Dress Rehearsal For 24-Hour Trading" is a title with no text, so there is nothing to test.
