## What it is

Timothy Masters' page for TSSB, a program he wrote with David Aronson (author of *Evidence-Based Technical Analysis*)
for testing trading systems with statistics before trading them. It is short, but it makes one of the most important
points in the whole set of materials.

## Everything it teaches

1. Test a system with strict statistics before risking money on it.
   > "Evidence-Based Technical Analysis (John Wiley and Sons, 2006) demonstrated the importance of using rigorous statistical techniques to analyze proposed trading systems before deploying them to trade real money."
2. **A walk-forward test alone is not enough.**
   > "Many developers believe that a basic walkforward test is sufficient to vet the performance of their proposed system. Unfortunately, this is far from true."
3. A good walk-forward result can still fail in live trading.
   > "It is easy to find a system that provides enticing walkforward performance but that goes on to fail miserably in actual trading."
4. The program is free, but no longer developed or hosted.
   > "this program, as well as its manual, are free."
   > "I have completely retired, and David has taken down the TSSB website. All major development has stopped."

## Claims we could test on our own data

Directly relevant. This repo picks strategies by walk-forward (chosen on older days, judged on newer ones). Masters says
that can still pass systems that fail. The repo's own Oct 20, 2026 result agrees: picks made −$0.23 a trade on unseen
days against −$0.22 for random entries. The page does not say what the extra tests are; that needs the TSSB manual.
