## What it is

The companion page for Carver's book *Leveraged Trading*. It lists free spreadsheets that work through the book's
calculations, chapter by chapter, and blog posts that go with each chapter.

## Everything it teaches

The page lists what the spreadsheets calculate; the calculations themselves are in the sheets, which were not
downloaded. The ones that touch this app most directly:

1. How to work out trading costs and the smallest position you can take.
   > "Cost calculation and minimum sizes (chapter 5, appendix B)"
2. How to size a position when opening it.
   > "Position opening calculations (chapter 6)"
3. How to check whether a stop has been hit.
   > "Has my stop been triggered? (chapter 6)"
4. A risk-adjusted performance measure.
   > "Performance ratio (chapter 11)"
5. Three trading-rule calculations: momentum, breakout and carry.
   > "Momentum trading rule calculation (chapter 6, chapter 8)"
