## Claims we could test on our own data

The page makes no claim, so there is nothing to confirm or refute from it. The titles point at ideas a backtest on this repo's Gate data could check once the posts themselves are read:

- "Turn of the Month Seasonality": split each coin's trades by whether they opened in the first or last few days of a month and compare profit a trade, after fees, against the rest.
- "Timing Luck": run the same strategy with its signal candles started at shifted offsets (a 4h strategy starting at 00:00 against 01:00 UTC, and so on) and see how far the result moves when only the start changes.
- "Back-test Reality Check": turn off one cost at a time (the 0.075% fee, the minute's buy-sell gap, funding) on a stored row and show each one's share of the result.
- "Channel Breakout" and "Conditional Channel Breakout": measure a breakout entry over the full grid (every coin, 15m to 1d) with and without a condition, beside random entries.
- "FX and Stop Loss": compare the same entries at several stop sizes, after fees.
