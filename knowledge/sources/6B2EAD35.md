## What it is

The front page of a website that sells a book about using AI and machine learning for trading, written by people
who run QuantConnect (a cloud backtesting platform), PredictNow, RocketEdge, Adaptive Investment Solutions and an AWS
AI team. It lists the book's nine chapter titles, says in one or two sentences per section what the book covers, and
quotes six reviewers. It gives no method, no rule, no code and no result. It is almost all sales copy, so this note
is short: every lesson below is a topic the page says the book covers, not something the page itself teaches.

1. The book covers the main kinds of machine learning, from simple to deep models.
   > "Explore fundamental concepts such as supervised and unsupervised learning, as well as advanced techniques like deep learning, neural networks, and recurrent neural networks."
   Where: the INTRODUCTION section ("Gain an Intuition for AI-Driven Trading").
2. It says these models are used to find patterns in market data.
   > "Learn how these methodologies can be applied to financial data to identify patterns, trends, and anomalies"
   Where: same section.
3. It has worked examples of three strategy types: buying back toward an average, following a move, and trading two related prices against each other.

   Where: the KNOWLEDGE section ("Apply Knowledge Through Real-World Case Studies").
4. It promises to cover the weak points and risks of AI strategies, not only the good ones.

## What it says on costs, overfitting, testing on unseen data, position size, leverage and stops

**Costs:** not covered. The page never mentions fees, the gap between buy and sell prices, or funding.

**Overfitting** (tuning a strategy until it fits the past by luck): not covered. The word "optimization" appears only
as a platform feature and as part of Chapter 8's title.

**Testing on unseen data:** not covered. Chapters 4 and 5 are named "Dataset Preparation" and "Model Choice,Training,
and Application", but the page says nothing about keeping some data back to test on.

**Position size:** not covered directly. Chapter 8's title ("AI for Risk Management and Optimization") may touch it,
and the platform section only says:
> "you can focus on generating alpha while effectively, managing risk and maximizing trading performance."

**Leverage:** not covered.

**Stops:** not covered. The closest thing is hedging (Chapter 7, "Better Hedging with Reinforcement Learning"), which
is a different tool.

## Claims we could test on our own data

None in a form we can run. The page names strategy types (mean reversion, momentum, statistical arbitrage) and says
indicator-based inputs can find "high-probability trading opportunities", but it gives no rule, no setting, no
timeframe and no result, so there is nothing exact to confirm or refute. If the chapter pages or the book's examples
were read and gave a concrete rule, that rule could be run through this repo's usual check: choose on older days,
judge on newer ones, after every fee, beside random entries on the same coins.

## What I did not read, and doubts

- I read every word of this one page. I did not read the chapter pages, the examples, the errata, the FAQ, the blog
  or the book, so the book's real methods, settings and results are unknown here. Everything in "Everything it
  teaches" is the page's description of the book, not a lesson shown on the page.
- The page is marketing. Nine of its links are "Get Your Copy" buttons to the paid book. The six reviews are short
  blurbs ("A must read if you want to use AI for building wealth."), not tests of the book's strategies.
- The authors run the companies the page links to (QuantConnect, PredictNow, RocketEdge, Adaptive Investment
  Solutions), so the platform advice comes from people who sell that platform.
- The $75 credit is described two ways: "Included" at the top, and earned by posting a photo of yourself reading the
  book further down.
- "high-probability trading opportunities" and "actionable insights" are promises with no number behind them.
- The saved text has three broken characters (shown as a replacement mark): in the word you'll, around "Quant of the Year",
  and before "Copyright". I avoided quoting across them.
