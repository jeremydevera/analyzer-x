## Claims we could test on our own data

The page makes no claim with a number, so nothing it says can be confirmed or refuted as stated. These are the IDEAS from its example list that this repo's crypto data could check, each judged on days the choice never saw, after every fee, beside random entries:

1. "Detecting trends in the crypto market" works: the Backtest v3 trend family and the Backtest v2 trend signals already measure this; the check is whether trend entries beat random entries on unseen days after the fee and the buy-sell gap (our past research says picks did not beat random, so this would be a re-check, not a new idea).
2. A stop that widens and narrows with recent price swings beats a fixed-percent stop: run the same signals with a stop set from recent swings (for example a multiple of average candle range) against the fixed stop/target grid on Backtest v2's minute-settled exits, same coins and days, unseen days only.
3. Predicting tomorrow's swings (calm vs wild periods): on daily candles, check whether a regime model predicts next-day price swings better than simply using yesterday's swings, over held-out months.
4. Putting money into calmer coins does better: compare per-trade results on the calm group against the liquid group in the v3 universe plan, per coin, on unseen days.
5. Trading cost can be predicted and avoided by timing: the per-minute order-book cost store could show whether a coin's cost at a given hour is predictable from earlier days, and whether skipping the predicted expensive minutes improves the result after costs.
6. Next day favors momentum or reversion, predictable by a classifier: on daily candles, train on older months and check the hit rate on later months against always guessing the more common answer.

Not testable here: stock splits, dividends, company accounts data and news sentiment (no such data in this repo), and the head-and-shoulders forex example (the exchange lists only 4 forex contracts).
