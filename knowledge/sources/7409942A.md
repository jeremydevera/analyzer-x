## What it is

This is the index page of Aswath Damodaran's online classes. It lists seven classes (Accounting, Statistics, Foundations of Finance, Corporate Finance Online, Valuation Online, Investment Philosophies Online, The Corporate Life Cycle), each with one or two sentences on what it covers and links to "My site" and "YouTube". It opens with the author's view that learning online is different from learning in a room. It teaches no trading method itself; it only points to the classes. It ends by pointing to a video and a blog post on which class to take in which order.

## Everything it teaches

1. Online classes need shorter sessions than a classroom. The author found a long lecture is hard to sit through online and says he is still learning how to teach this way.
   > "an 80-minute lecture, which may be palatable in a regular class, is much more difficult to take online"
   Where: the opening paragraph, above the class table.

2. Accounting: how to read financial statements and use their numbers as the raw material for corporate finance and valuation. The author warns he takes shortcuts a strict accountant would not.
   > "how best to read financial statements and use the data from those statements as raw material in corporate finance and valuation"
   Where: class table, row "Accounting".

3. Statistics: applied statistics for the needs of finance and investing, built around turning data into information.
   > "It starts with vision of statistics as the key to converting data to information"
   Where: class table, row "Statistics".

4. Foundations of Finance: a short first course on the basic ideas of finance, for someone with no finance background, before the corporate finance and valuation classes.
   > "from what comprises a business to how to measure cash flows and risk to the time value of money to the basics of valuing stocks, bonds and options"
   Where: class table, row "Foundations of Finance".

5. Corporate Finance Online: the author's full MBA corporate finance class, cut into short online sessions, to go with his book Applied Corporate Finance (4th Edition).
   > "composed of 36 sessions of 12-15 minutes apiece that covers what I do in a regular MBA class"
   Where: class table, row "Corporate Finance Online".

6. Valuation Online: the MBA valuation class with each long class replaced by a short one, and a test after each class.
   > "replaces the 80 minute classes with shorter ones (15-20 minutes), with post-class tests"
   Where: class table, row "Valuation Online".

7. Investment Philosophies Online: a class taught only online, never in a classroom, tied to his book on Investment Philosophies.
   > "This is a class that I have not taught in the classroom but decided to teach entirely as an online class"
   Where: class table, row "Investment Philosophies Online".

8. The Corporate Life Cycle: how businesses age, and what that means for how they decide, how they are valued, how to invest in them and how they are run. It goes with his book on the topic.
   > "I look at how businesses age (move through the life cycle) and the implications for how they make decisions"
   Where: class table, row "The Corporate Life Cycle".

9. Which class to take first: the author points to a video and a blog post that give the order and content of his classes.
   > "I have a YouTube video taking you through the sequencing and content of these classes"
   Where: the paragraph under the class table.
