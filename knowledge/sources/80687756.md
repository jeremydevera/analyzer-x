## What it is

This is the author's page for his book "Analysis of Financial Time Series, Third Edition" (Wiley-Interscience, 2010). It does not teach the book's content in words: it is a list, chapter by chapter, of the data files used in the text and in the exercises, plus a few program files (R, S, RATS, SCA and Fortran). The page names the software the book uses, links an errata file, and says that exercise solutions go only to instructors. Every data set on it is US stock, index, bond, interest-rate, exchange-rate or economic data, mostly ending in 2008 or 2009; there is no crypto data and no trading rule.

Note: the page only names topics and data. Each "lesson" below says, in plain words, what the page states about that item and nothing more.
