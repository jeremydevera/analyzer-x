## Claims we could test on our own data

The page itself makes no claim that could be tested. It gives titles, not rules.

There is one lead, and it comes only from the tut2 fetch-tool summary, not the author's words. That summary says Mish Schneider's six phases are set by where the price sits against its 50-day and 200-day averages. We could test this as a filter on our daily candles. For example, do long trades opened when the 50-day average is above the 200-day and the price is above both make more after fees than long trades opened in the other phases, on the same strategies? Before relying on it, read the real episode page, because the summary may be wrong.
